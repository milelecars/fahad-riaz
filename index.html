About Fahad Riaz.

Based in Dubai, UAE

My father began importing cars in the 1980s. In 1995, our family established operations in Dubai with one clear vision: build something that lasts.

Today, Milele Motors stocks over 4,000 vehicles across Dubai and Antwerp, exporting nearly 10,000 cars annually to more than 50 countries.

“Build systems. Create leverage. Expand across continents.”

Fahad Riaz is a Dubai-based entrepreneur recognized for driving record-breaking sales and market presence across Milele's multi-vertical ventures. His edge isn't hype; it's turning complex challenges into structured systems that scale, backed by disciplined growth and long-term value creation across international markets including Africa, Asia, and the Middle East.
